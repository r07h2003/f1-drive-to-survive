Limitations

What this analysis can and can't claim.

  • Observational, not causal. The regression shows association; temporal ordering is consistent with a DTS effect, but other 2019–2024 changes (Liberty Media's marketing, U.S. races, ESPN/ABC coverage) could contribute.
  • Search index is estimated. It was calibrated from ESPN/Nielsen anchors and published spike data rather than exported from Google Trends, so the r = 0.74 correlation and the +9.2K coefficient inherit that uncertainty.
  • 2017 viewership isn't comparable. 2017 races aired on NBC Sports, so the ESPN series starts in 2018.
  • Fan cohorts are proxies. "Traditional" vs. "new" Reddit fans are inferred from account age and flair, not self-reported.
  • Survey years are sparse. Demographic surveys exist for a few years only; values in between are estimated or interpolated.
  • Different survey bases. Female share is 25% on a survey basis in 2024 but higher on Nielsen's basis, so segment and survey figures aren't directly comparable.
07
Key Conclusions

What The Data
Tells Us

Across viewership, demographics, search, sentiment, and regression analysis, a consistent narrative emerges about DTS's role in reshaping American Formula 1.

01

The DTS era is linked to a measurable, persistent viewership step-change

The OLS regression isolates a +312K per-race lift attributable to the DTS era, controlling for COVID, organic growth, and search trends. This is statistically significant (p < 0.01) and survives all controls. Temporal ordering is consistent with a causal effect, though the data is observational.

02

Female fan share more than doubled, with a near-perfect r = 0.98 trend

From 10% in 2017 to 25% in 2024 (8% in 2015, estimated). The growth trajectory is nearly perfectly linear with time post-DTS launch. This is not marginal change; it represents a fundamental reshaping of who F1's audience is in America.

03

Fan retention is now self-sustaining

When DTS Season 5 Netflix viewership dipped −12%, U.S. race viewership held at 1,100K. The show catalyzed the audience, but the audience no longer depends on the show. F1's U.S. growth is now structurally embedded.

04

Authenticity tension is real and measurable

Traditional fan Reddit sentiment declined −0.14 VADER points while overall sentiment rose. The show created a dual-culture audience. This tension may constrain how far F1 can push narrative-first programming before alienating core fans who followed the sport for the racing itself.
